# Knowledge Sources

The CSM Official AI Assistant is powered by multiple knowledge sources to provide accurate and context-aware responses to users.

## Primary Knowledge Sources

### 1. Official Capital Smart Motors Website
The assistant indexes and retrieves information from the official Capital Smart Motors website, including:

- Company information
- Electric vehicle models
- Product specifications
- Booking information
- Test drive details
- Warranty information
- Dealership information
- Contact information
- Gallery pages

---

### 2. Custom Knowledge Files

Two custom knowledge files were uploaded into the Botpress Knowledge Base.

#### Core Knowledge Base

botpress_core_csm_knowledge_fast_upload.txt

Contains detailed business information, FAQs, company services, vehicle information, and customer support content.

#### Essential Answers Dataset

botpress_essential_answers_first_upload.txt

Contains predefined responses for frequently asked customer questions to improve response speed and consistency.

---

### 3. Website Crawling

The official CSM website was crawled using Botpress Website Knowledge Source.

Indexed pages include:

- Home
- About
- Contact
- Gallery
- Vehicle Pages
- Company Information
- Smart Mobility Content

---

### 4. Web Search

Web Search was enabled to improve response quality for information that may require updated public information while prioritizing the internal knowledge base whenever possible.
